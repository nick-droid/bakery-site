OUR STORY

Meet Cora — yes, she's real!

She still scoops the first tray every morning in Kilimani. Here is how a stack of 3x5 recipe cards became Nairobi's favorite bake shop.

Hi! I'm Cora, founder of Cora & Crumb.

Every day as a child, I would come home from school to enjoy my grandmother's company over a glass of fresh milk and warm cookies. That simple ritual taught me that baking is about bringing people together.

When we started Cora & Crumb in Kilimani, Nairobi, we made a promise: zero artificial shortcuts, European butter, real chocolate, and handcrafted care in every batch.

"If it wouldn't have been good enough for my grandmothers, it doesn't leave the kitchen."

— CORA • FOUNDER

HOW WE BAKE

Three rules, no exceptions

Real butter, always

European butter, real vanilla, proper chocolate chunks. No shortenings or artificial flavors — ever.

Small batches only

Every tray is mixed, hand-scooped and baked fresh daily in Kilimani. When we sell out, we're done for the day.

Recipes written by hand

Tested and tweaked over decades. Our original cards still sit on the shelf in the kitchen.
